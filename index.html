Crosswords on any theme, made for you

Pick a theme, a language and a difficulty. Wordlai asks an AI model for the words and clues, builds the grid, and you solve it.

Get started

Any theme

Science, geography, cinema, your own idea… every puzzle is generated on the spot.

20 languages

Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic, Thai and Vietnamese scripts. Puzzle language is independent of the app language.

Your AI provider

Use your own API key with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral and more. Groq offers a free tier to start.

Private by design

No account, no wordlai server, no ads or trackers. Your puzzles stay on your phone.

How it works

  1. Connect a provider. The app guides you through getting an API key; a free Groq key is enough to begin.
  2. Choose a theme. Set the language, difficulty and how many words from your earlier puzzles to review.
  3. Solve. Tap a clue, type the answer. Stuck? Use a hint (it costs a few points).
  4. Keep going. Browse past puzzles, pin words to your bucket list, follow your stats.

Good to know

Wordlai sends requests straight from your phone to the AI provider you configure, using your own key. Generating a puzzle uses very little of that provider's allowance. See the privacy policy for details.
