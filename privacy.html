Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy covers the Wordlai Android app (package dev.wordlai.wordlai). In short: Wordlai has no server, no account system, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect your data.

Who is responsible

Wordlai is developed by an individual developer. Contact: mart2010.l@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Your API keys, kept in Android's secure storage.
  • Your preferences (providers and models, languages, themes, display options).
  • Your puzzles and progress, scores and statistics.
  • Your word bank: words and clues from past puzzles, bucket-list and excluded words.

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device.

Data sent to third parties

The app connects to the internet only to do the following. Nothing passes through a Wordlai server.

AI providers you configure

To generate puzzles, the app sends requests directly from your phone to the provider(s) you set up (for example Groq, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, Alibaba, or a custom endpoint you enter). These requests contain your API key (for authentication), the theme, language and difficulty you chose, and words and clues needed for generation and automatic checking (for example, words already used, to avoid repeats). They do not contain your name, contact details or device identifiers beyond what any internet connection reveals (such as your IP address). How a provider handles requests is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy; please review them.

Dictionary service

To check generated words and clues, the app looks up single words, with the language code, on freedictionaryapi.com, a free Wiktionary-backed service that needs no key. That service sees the looked-up word and your IP address.

Links you open

If you tap a link (for example, a provider's API-key page), it opens in your browser and is subject to that site's policy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of data, because we hold none.
  • The app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Children

Wordlai is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can delete a stored API key, puzzles or words inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it. To revoke a key, do so in your provider's console.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: mart2010.l@gmail.com.
